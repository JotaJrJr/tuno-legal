Part 1 — Privacy Policy

1. Summary

The app works without sign-up and without an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or payment data, we do not use your device's location (GPS), and we do not run our own server: we do not receive or store data about you. Your favorites, your history and your preferences stay only on your device. To find and play radio stations, the app connects to some third-party services, and the app is free because it shows Google AdMob ads, which are not personalized. All of this is described below.

2. Data that stays only on your device

  • Favorites and recently played: the stations you marked and the last ones you listened to.
  • Preferences: Tuno's color and accessories, the “Play when opening the app” option, and the last station you listened to.
  • Temporary copy of the catalog: public data of the stations already searched (name, country, audio address), so the app opens faster.

This data is not sent to us or to third parties.

3. Data sent to third-party services

To work, the app communicates directly with the services below. As with any internet access, each one receives the IP address of your connection and basic technical data (for example, the app's name and version). None of them receives your name or email.

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), an open catalog of radio stations maintained by the community: it receives the searches you make (name, country, state, language, genre), the coordinates of the map point where you drop Tuno, to find nearby stations, and the identifier of the station you play, which counts toward a public click count.
  • Nominatim, from the OpenStreetMap Foundation (privacy policy): it receives the coordinates of the map point, to show the place name (“You are in…”).
  • OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org): provides the map images of the region you are viewing.
  • The radio stations: when you play a station, the app connects to the audio server of that station, which receives the technical data of the connection, as with any website. Each station has its own rules.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC), to show ads: it receives device and connection data (model, operating system, language, IP address), ad interaction data and, on Android, the device's advertising identifier. This data is used to show ads, limit repetition, measure and prevent fraud. See how Google uses data and the Google privacy policy.

The coordinates sent are those of the point you choose on the map, not your device's location.

4. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, device location (GPS), contacts, photos, microphone audio or financial data. The app has no user accounts and does not use usage analytics tools. On iPhone, the app does not ask permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

5. How data is used

  • To search for radio stations, show the place name on the map and play live audio.
  • To keep your favorites, history and Tuno's look on your device.
  • To show ads that keep the app free.

6. Sale and sharing

We do not sell personal data. Since we have no server, we have no data about you to share; sending data to the services in item 3 happens directly from your device, so the app works and shows ads.

7. Ads

The app shows a banner on the map screen and, only if you choose, a video in Settings (“Support Tuno”). The ads are provided by Google AdMob and are not personalized based on your profile. Where the law requires it (for example, in the European Union and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and Apple Advertising).

8. Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not collect data from children under 13. The programming of the radio stations is the responsibility of each station and may not be suitable for all ages.

9. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it: in Settings, “Clear recently played” erases the history; uninstalling the app erases everything. Third-party services, including Google AdMob, keep data according to their own policies.

10. Security

Communications with the catalog, the map and the place search use a secure connection (HTTPS). Some stations only offer audio over a regular connection (HTTP); in that case the app receives only the station's audio, without sending data about you.

11. Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13.709/2018) and similar laws elsewhere, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data. Since the app's data stays only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly by clearing the history or uninstalling the app. For questions or requests, contact us at jotapereirajunior@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the version in effect.

13. Contact

Part 3 — Support

Found a problem, have a suggestion, or a station is wrong? Write to jotapereirajunior@gmail.com with your device model, the station and what happened.

The station doesn't play or shows “No signal”

The station's broadcast may be off the air or unstable. Turn the dial or use the − and + buttons on the orange radio to try another station in the area. Also check that you are connected to the internet.

Tuno doesn't find stations in a place

Many stations in the catalog do not say where they are. That is why the app searches near the point, then in the state, then in a wider area and, finally, in the whole country. In places with no registered stations, try a nearby city or use the Search tab.

How to save a station

Tap the heart on the station, in the Search list or in Favorites. Your favorites and recently played stations are in the Favorites tab.

Sleep timer and play when opening

In the Settings tab: the “Sleep timer” pauses the radio after the chosen time, and “Play when opening the app” brings Tuno back to the last station you played and plays it.

How to change Tuno's look

In the Settings tab, choose the color and the accessories (hat, glasses and scarf). Tap Tuno to make him wave.

Does the app work without internet?

No. Radio is broadcast over the internet, so you need to be connected (Wi-Fi or mobile data). Without a connection, Tuno looks sad and tells you; when the internet comes back, he looks for the station again on his own.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Tuno free. They are not personalized. If you want to help, tap “Support Tuno” in Settings to watch a short video.
